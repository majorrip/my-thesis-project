\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

As artificial intelligence moves toward decentralized deployment across local edge nodes, mitigating hallucination and latent representation collapse without relying on cloud-based computation remains a critical challenge. Centralized architectures impose extreme communication latencies and data privacy risks, whereas conventional lightweight models suffer from severe representation degradation when executed on resource-constrained micro-architectures. 

This thesis introduces \textbf{EdgeVerify}, a multi-agent, Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture (JEPA) designed for localized execution across Dew and Edge computing tiers. Rather than generating high-dimensional token sequences or raw pixel reconstructions, EdgeVerify projects context representations directly into an abstract joint-embedding space. To prevent dimensional collapse during localized gradient updates, we incorporate VicReg-inspired variance and covariance regularization directly into layer-wise distillation loss calculations. Experimental evaluation demonstrates that the proposed localized architecture preserves representation entropy, achieves stable convergence (local loss $\sim 0.87$), and eliminates raw data transmission to third-party clouds, establishing a verifiable framework for data sovereignty and real-time edge verification.
